# Product ranges

## Fasteners

Bolts, nuts, washers and anchors in zinc-plated, galvanised and stainless finishes. Sold in boxes of
100 or 500. Stainless items carry a 20% premium over zinc-plated.

## Fittings

Hinges, brackets, clamps and pipe fittings. Brackets are sold individually; hinges in pairs.

## Seals and gaskets

Rubber and silicone gaskets, O-rings and valve seals. Silicone items are made to order and cannot be
returned unless faulty.

## Bearings and springs

Ball bearings, roller bearings and compression springs. Bearings are sold individually; springs in
packs of 10.

## Pricing rules

- Prices are per unit or per pack as listed, excluding tax.
- Volume pricing: 5% off order lines over 10,000, and 8% off lines over 50,000.
- Volume discounts and the early payment discount can be combined.
- Prices are reviewed quarterly; quoted prices are held for 14 days.
