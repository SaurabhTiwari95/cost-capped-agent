# Frequently asked questions

**Can I change an order after placing it?**
Yes, until it has been picked. Picking usually starts within 4 working hours of the order.

**Do you deliver on weekends?**
No. Deliveries run Monday to Friday. Orders placed on Friday after 2 p.m. are picked on Monday.

**How do I get credit terms?**
Pay for your first three orders in advance, then apply. See the payment terms in the handbook.

**I was charged a late fee. Can it be removed?**
A late fee can be waived once in any 12-month period if the overdue amount is paid within 7 days of
the fee being added. Ask your account manager.

**Can I return a cut-to-length item I ordered by mistake?**
No. Cut-to-length and made-to-order items can only be returned if they are faulty.
