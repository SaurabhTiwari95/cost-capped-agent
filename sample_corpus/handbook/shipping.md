# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times

| Zone | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| Metro | 2 working days | next working day |
| Regional | 4 working days | 2 working days |
| Remote | 7 working days | not available |

## Freight charges

- Orders over 25,000 ship free on standard delivery to metro and regional zones.
- Express delivery is charged at actual cost plus 10%.
- Remote zone freight is quoted per order.

## Damaged in transit

Inspect goods on arrival. Note any visible damage on the delivery receipt before signing, and report
it within 48 hours. Damage reported later than 48 hours is handled as a faulty-item claim (see the
returns policy).
