# Returns policy

## What can be returned

- Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within **30 days of delivery**.
- Made-to-order and cut-to-length items cannot be returned unless they are faulty.
- Clearance items are sold as seen and cannot be returned.

## Faulty items

- Report a fault within 14 days of delivery with photos and the invoice number.
- Acme will replace the item or issue a credit note, at the customer's choice.
- Acme pays return freight for faulty items only.

## Restocking fee

A 10% restocking fee applies to non-faulty returns over 5,000 in value. There is no fee for
returns under 5,000.

## How refunds are paid

- Customers on credit terms receive a credit note against their account.
- Customers who paid in advance are refunded to the original payment method within 7 working days
  of the return being inspected.
