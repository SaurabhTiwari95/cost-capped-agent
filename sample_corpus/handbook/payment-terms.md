# Payment terms

## Standard terms

- New customers pay in advance for their first three orders.
- After three paid orders, a customer may apply for credit terms.
- Standard credit terms are **30 days from the invoice date**.
- Customers with more than 24 months of on-time payment may be offered 45 days.

## Credit limits

- The starting credit limit is 50,000.
- The limit is reviewed every six months. It can rise by up to 50% at each review if every invoice
  in the period was paid on time.
- Orders that would take a customer over their limit are held until a payment arrives.

## Late payment

- A reminder is sent 3 days after the due date.
- After 15 days overdue, new orders are put on hold.
- After 30 days overdue, a late fee of 1.5% of the outstanding amount is added each month.
- After 60 days overdue, the account is passed to the collections team and credit terms end.

## Early payment discount

Customers on credit terms receive a **2% discount** if they pay in full within 10 days of the invoice
date. The discount does not apply to freight charges.
